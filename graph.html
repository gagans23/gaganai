How to read this

Nodes are entities the radar has tracked — frontier labs, big tech, chipmakers, banks, GCC champions, places and cross-cutting concepts. Bigger means it has appeared in more verified signals.

Lines are co-appearances: two entities named in the same story. Thicker lines mean it keeps happening — that is a thread worth watching, not a coincidence.

Colour is the entity type; the panel shows the dominant layer (L1 energy → L5 agents) and the themes each entity is driving. This is the same engine that writes the month-end connect-the-dots brief.
