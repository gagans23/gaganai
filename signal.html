04 / Trust is inspectable

Show the work.

Collection and classification are automated. The conclusions, actions, and falsification criteria are editorial. This page organises the published ledger; it does not invent answers.

What makes a story a signal?

Five tests: mandate, money, machinery, corroboration, and a change in buying criteria. A signal must change a real constraint. Shared mentions alone are not evidence of a causal relationship.

How is momentum measured?

Momentum compares recorded activity across consecutive time windows. Counts describe this collection, not the whole market.

Can I inspect what was rejected?

Yes. Choose “Rejected by the gate” in the evidence feed. Rejection means the structural tests were not met, not that the reporting is false.
