The Noise Gate · A mental model

Most of it is noise. Here's what it points toward.

A flood of AI news arrives every day. Almost none of it changes anything. This page runs the month through a gate — keeping only what shifts structure — and shows the two or three things it's actually pointing toward, and why.

How the gate works

A stimulus is noise by default. It only becomes signal if it changes structure — if it trips at least one of five tests. Demos, launches with no buyer, predictions and horse-race takes trip nothing, so they decay. Signals that point the same way bind into an attractor: a direction the month is bending toward.

MandateA rule turns from suggested to required.
MoneySomeone attaches a price or a threshold.
MachineryA one-off becomes a permanent institution.
CorroborationIndependent actors make the same move.
Criterion-shiftWhat buyers are judged on changes.
