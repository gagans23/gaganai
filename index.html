What I Believe

AI becomes powerful when it becomes governable.

Model access is not the moat. Institutional trust is.

Agents need authority, memory, observability, and accountability before they need more demos.

Judgment does not disappear in an AI operating model. It moves into architecture, governance, and design.

The best technology leaders are translators between possibility, risk, capital, and human behavior.

In regulated enterprises, leadership is the ability to make ambition legible to risk, compliance, engineering, and the board.
