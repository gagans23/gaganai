About this book

A free seven-part illustrated field guide that rebuilds how modern AI actually works — for the curious non-specialist. No equations, no hype. Real depth, drawn from first principles: from how a model predicts the next word, through attention and training, to how agents get tools, remember, and act on their own. The understatements are deliberate; the depth is real.

Seven parts, end to end

  1. 01FoundationsWhat it is, and how it turns text into text.
  2. 02TeachingHow a feral predictor becomes a helpful assistant.
  3. 03ReasoningHow a machine that only blurted learned to think before it speaks.
  4. 04MeasuringHow you grade something that’s fluent on purpose.
  5. 05Agents I — Giving the Machine HandsWhat turns an answer-engine into something that acts.
  6. 06Agents II — A World of Cooperating AgentsTools, memory, and agents that talk to each other.
  7. 07Under the Hood & Field ReferenceThe stack beneath it all, and a plain-language glossary.
