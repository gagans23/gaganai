to an institution that can act.

My role connects four decisions: where to invest, what to build, how much authority to give a system, and how to make it useful in daily work. Each shapes the others. A platform decision becomes an operating decision; a governance decision changes what the technology can deliver.

02 / Architecture

Build the capability the institution can reuse.

I led the ground-up platform build, connecting cloud foundations, model access, context, identity and agent execution into shared capabilities.

03 / Governance

Make authority and accountability explicit.

I developed the group’s AI governance model jointly with the CISO, linking the platform to how agents are approved, monitored and controlled.

The question I bringWhat may this system do, what evidence supports that authority, and who is responsible when it needs intervention?

04 / Delivery

Carry the idea through to the operation.

I bring forward-deployed engineers, AI engineers, data scientists and cloud specialists together around production workflows and the people using them.

The question I bringHas the work become better for the people doing it—and can the institution keep delivering that improvement?

I led the ground-up build of our AI and agentic platform. We own the architecture connecting identity, models, context, tools and execution—so teams can take agents into governed banking workflows.

We built these foundations using cloud services and models as components. The responsibility includes how the parts work together, how they are controlled and how teams use them in production.

Inside the platform

Governance & identity
The governance model, agent identity and access controls that establish what an agent is allowed to do. I developed the group’s AI governance model jointly with the CISO.
Runtime & orchestration
The execution layer where agents run and the orchestration that connects models, tools and workflow steps into an operating service.
Context & model access
Context management and the LLM gateway: foundations for supplying agents with relevant information and managing how applications access models.
Cloud foundations
Work across AWS and Azure, connecting the cloud infrastructure to the platform and the needs of teams delivering production AI.

Our teams use these foundations in production across financial crime, trade finance, KYC, onboarding and servicing. These workflows bring the four layers together: a business need, a working system, explicit controls and an operating team.

What the work made possible~$5M · First-year portfolio efficiency

Across the operations transformation portfolio, selected use cases cut turnaround and handling times by up to 75%; trade operations cleared 8,000+ trade credits in one quarter. These are outcomes delivered by our teams across the portfolio, supported by the platform and the changes to the workflows around it.
