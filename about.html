01 / Research 2007–2009

Question the answer.

Engineering taught me to take systems apart. Financial research taught me to examine the assumptions holding them together. At Copal Partners, now part of Moody’s Analytics, I worked on due diligence and scenario models for global companies.

What the work made possibleFinancial due diligence · Scenario modelling

My starting point was learning to ask what would have to be true—and what could make it false.

What I carry forwardAn answer is only as useful as the assumptions you can defend.

02 / Capital markets 2009–2020

Understand what the decision sets in motion.

Eleven years at Bank of America brought me into debt capital markets and then fintech innovation. Working with US and European bankers on leveraged finance meant connecting analysis to decisions about capital. Fintech widened the question: how could technology change the institution making those decisions?

What the work made possible11 years · Debt capital markets → Fintech

I began to see technology through the people, incentives and risks that determine whether it gets used.

What I carry forwardTechnical possibility needs commercial judgment.

03 / Business intelligence 2020–2021

Get the insight to the person who needs it.

At First Abu Dhabi Bank, I led business intelligence, enterprise Power BI adoption and a centralised financial reporting platform. The task was to make information available soon enough to change a decision.

What the work made possibleT−10 → T−3 days · Financial reporting

Analytics and automation use cases contributed AED 2M in revenue growth and AED 1M in cost savings. Insight became something we could measure in the operation.

What I carry forwardSpeed matters when it gives someone time to act.

04 / Customer AI 2021–2023

Let use be the test.

As Senior AI Product Manager at FAB, I worked on conversational banking, voice biometrics and customer-call analytics. Engineering, design, operations, risk and compliance all had to come together around the person trying to get something done.

What the work made possible350,000 customers · Conversational chatbot

The chatbot handled 100,000+ transactions a month. Our Voice of Customer platform analysed 200,000 monthly calls. At that scale, the important questions become practical: can people finish the task, and can the service keep its promise?

What I carry forwardA product earns its value in someone else’s hands.

05 / Enterprise AI 2023–Present

Build the institution’s ability to act.

Today I lead AI and agentic platforms at FAB, with a function of around 150. My responsibility spans strategy, architecture, investment choices and advising the Group CEO and executive committee. Our teams have put agentic use cases into production across financial crime, trade finance, KYC, onboarding and servicing.

What the work made possible~$5M · First-year portfolio efficiency

I established the group’s AI governance model jointly with the CISO and built the governed platform for deploying and controlling agents. Selected use cases cut turnaround and handling times by up to 75%; trade operations cleared 8,000+ trade credits in one quarter. These are team outcomes across the transformation portfolio.

What I carry forwardCapability scales when responsibility scales with it.

06 / Open work An ongoing practice

Build it. Examine it. Revise the thinking.

That same discipline runs through my public work. Agent OS and Ninja Harness explore how traces, evaluation, memory and approvals fit into working systems. The Philosophical Ledger is where I examine the institutional questions those systems raise.

What the work made possibleEssays · Open systems · Research

Writing makes my reasoning visible. Building gives me something concrete to examine. Together, they keep learning part of the job.

What I carry forwardKeep the argument close enough to the work to be challenged.
