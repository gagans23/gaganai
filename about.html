The thread through the work

My career has widened the question.

First: is the analysis sound? Then: will the transaction work? Later: can this product serve hundreds of thousands of people? Today: how does an entire institution build the capacity to use AI responsibly?

Over more than eighteen years, I have moved from financial research and debt capital markets into fintech, analytics, AI products and enterprise platforms. Each move added a different kind of responsibility. Understanding the technology was one part. Learning how people, capital, risk and execution fit together became the larger work.

That is the perspective I bring: an engineer’s interest in how things work, a banker’s attention to consequences, and a builder’s commitment to making an idea useful.

Five chapters / A widening field of responsibility

How I built my range.

01 / FoundationsEngineering · Finance · Research

Learn to look beneath the answer.

I studied Electronics & Communications at Thapar, then finance and international business at Asia Pacific Institute of Management. My early work at Copal Partners, now part of Moody’s Analytics, involved financial due diligence, scenario modelling and research on global companies.

It was a foundation in examining assumptions: understanding how a result is produced, what supports it, and how it changes when the conditions change. That habit still matters when the output comes from an AI system.

2007–2009 · Financial research and scenario modelling

02 / ConsequencesBank of America · 2009–2020

Put judgment close to capital.

Across eleven years at Bank of America, my work spanned debt capital markets and fintech innovation. I worked with US and European bankers on leveraged finance origination and execution, connecting company analysis, credit conditions and financing decisions.

The move into fintech expanded my attention from the transaction to the institution behind it: how a bank evaluates new technology, aligns teams and changes the way work gets done. Commercial judgment and technical possibility had to meet in the same conversation.

Mumbai & New York · Debt capital markets → Fintech innovation

03 / VisibilityFirst Abu Dhabi Bank · 2020–2021

Make better decisions easier to reach.

At FAB, I began by leading business intelligence. The work included enterprise Power BI adoption and a centralised financial reporting platform. Reporting moved from T−10 to T−3 days; analytics and automation use cases contributed AED 2 million in revenue growth and AED 1 million in cost savings.

This was a practical bridge from insight to execution. A useful analytical system has to reach the people making decisions, in time for the answer to matter.

Reporting cycle · T−10 → T−3 days

04 / AdoptionFirst Abu Dhabi Bank · 2021–2023

Build for the person on the other end.

As Senior AI Product Manager, I moved closer to the customer: conversational banking, voice biometrics and tools for understanding customer calls. The role meant bringing engineering, design, operations, risk and compliance into a shared product roadmap.

The conversational chatbot reached 350,000 customers and handled more than 100,000 transactions a month. A Voice of Customer platform analysed 200,000 monthly calls. At that volume, usefulness becomes concrete: whether someone can complete a task, be understood and get the help they need.

350,000 customers · 100,000+ monthly chatbot transactions

05 / InstitutionFirst Abu Dhabi Bank · 2023–Present

Build the capacity behind the capability.

Today I lead AI and agentic platforms at FAB, with a function of around 150. My remit spans enterprise strategy, platform architecture, build-or-buy decisions and advice to the Group CEO and executive committee on AI’s value and risk.

Our teams have brought agentic use cases into production across financial crime, trade finance, KYC, onboarding and servicing. Alongside that portfolio, I established the group’s AI governance model jointly with the CISO and built the governed platform through which agents are deployed, orchestrated and controlled.

The responsibility now is to make those pieces work together: people who can deliver, technology that can scale, and controls that make accountability part of everyday operation.

Enterprise AI strategy · Production platforms · Governance · Multidisciplinary leadership

What the journey has changed in me

A broader view. A builder’s discipline.

Judgment

Start with the consequence.

Capital markets trained my attention on what a decision sets in motion. In AI, I ask who depends on the output, what happens if it is wrong, and what evidence earns the right to act.

Translation

Bring the whole room into the work.

A product takes shape across business, engineering, operations and risk. Leadership means making the choices clear enough for people with different responsibilities to move together.

Accountability

Make trust something you can inspect.

Approvals, evaluation, memory and traces are part of the system I want to build. They connect what an agent does to the people responsible for its consequences.

Learning

Keep doing the work of a student.

My path has required repeated learning across disciplines. Research, technical study, writing and hands-on building keep my thinking open to challenge as my responsibilities grow.
